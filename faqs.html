FAQs

Answers to the questions we get asked most. Still wondering about something? Drop us a message anytime.

What is BMJ Creative Studio?

BMJ Creative Studio is a design and web studio run by Qurat Ul Ain. I create practical digital work, including graphic design, social media content, invitation cards, resumes, brand kits, and writing for websites and profiles. Simple business websites are coming soon.

Who can hire you?

Anyone! I work with individuals, job seekers, freelancers, startups, and small businesses. Whether you need a single polished resume, a set of social media designs, or a brand kit, I scale my approach to fit your needs. No project is too small.

How much do you charge?

Prices depend on the service and are quoted per task, so you know the exact amount before any work starts. See the Services page for starting prices. Larger projects are quoted individually, and ongoing or custom help is quoted individually.

How fast will I get my work?

Small tasks like resumes, invitation cards, or social posts usually take 1 to 3 days. Bigger projects like a brand kit take about 3 to 5 days. I'll always confirm the exact delivery time before starting.

How do I place an order or get started?

Send me a message through the contact page (WhatsApp or email) and tell me what you need. I'll reply with a clear price and timeline, and we can start once you approve.

When do I need to pay?

For small tasks, payment is made upfront before I start. For bigger projects, like a full website, you pay 50% upfront to get started and the remaining 50% when the work is done.

How can I pay you?

I accept SadaPay and direct bank transfer, as well as EasyPaisa and JazzCash.

What if I need changes or don't like the final work?

Every project includes 2 free rounds of changes, so we can adjust things until you're happy with the result. Extra rounds after that are charged at 15% of the project price each.

What if I need something that isn't listed on your site?

Just ask! I'm happy to take on custom requests that fit my skills, and I'll tell you honestly if something is outside what I can do well.

Can I work with you or join your team?

Interested in working with BMJ Creative Studio? Send your CV or portfolio through the contact page and tell me which role you're interested in (design, development, marketing, or outreach). I'm building the team gradually and will reply when a project matches your skills. Payment terms are agreed in writing before each project starts.
